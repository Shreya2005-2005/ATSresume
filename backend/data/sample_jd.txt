Backend Engineer — Platform Team
Company: Quillhaven Analytics

About the role:
Quillhaven Analytics is hiring a Backend Engineer to join our Platform team, building the
APIs and infrastructure that power our customer-facing analytics product. You will
work closely with SRE to operate our services in production.

Requirements:
- 5+ years of professional software engineering experience
- 5+ years of production Kubernetes experience, including operating and debugging
  live clusters
- Strong proficiency in Python and experience building REST APIs (FastAPI or
  similar)
- Experience with PostgreSQL or another relational database
- Solid understanding of CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure-as-code
- Excellent communication skills and ability to work cross-functionally

Nice to have:
- Experience with Go
- Familiarity with the Helm ecosystem
- AWS or other public cloud certification

Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and operate REST APIs serving our analytics dashboard
- Own production Kubernetes workloads: deployments, scaling, incident response
- Improve CI/CD pipelines for faster, safer releases
- Collaborate with frontend and data teams on new features
